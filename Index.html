About

Respect for every person who walks in.

Dr. Brian R. McDonald approaches therapy with deep respect for each client. He believes the answer to a person’s problems ultimately lies within that person, and his role is to help you explore the thoughts and feelings that may be keeping life from feeling fulfilling.

He holds a Ph.D. in Counselor Education from The College of William & Mary and has served as an adjunct professor of counseling at Johns Hopkins University and George Mason University.
